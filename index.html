From first visit to the final switch.

Four stages, usually six to ten weeks for a home. We stay on the project until every scene is programmed and you know how to use it.

  1. 1

    Night survey

    We visit after dark, measure the light you have now and ask how each room is actually used.

  2. 2

    On-site mock-up

    Temporary fittings go up so you see the effect in your own space before anything is bought.

  3. 3

    Specification

    A drawing set and fitting schedule your electrician can follow, priced in three budgets.

  4. 4

    Commissioning

    We aim every fitting, set the scenes and hand over a one-page guide to your controls.
